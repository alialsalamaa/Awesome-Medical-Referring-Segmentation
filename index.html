Survey structure

Explore by imaging setting

The taxonomy connects each imaging setting to its relevant datasets and papers. A study can appear in more than one setting when its evaluation crosses modalities.

01

2D medical imaging

e.g., X-ray, ultrasound, fundus, dermoscopy, endoscopy, and 2D slices.

02

3D volumetric imaging

e.g., CT, MRI, PET, and volumetric ultrasound benchmarks and tasks.

03

Video and sequences

e.g., surgical video, endoscopy sequences, and temporally linked frames.

04

Pathology and microscopy

e.g., histopathology, cytology, and microscopy images across scales.
